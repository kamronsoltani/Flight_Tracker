FlightTracker phone control

Bluetooth was dropped — classic ESP32 IRAM is full with the LED matrix driver. Control runs over Wi‑Fi instead.

  1. Join the same network as the panel (Berkeley-IoT or your hotspot).
  2. Open Serial once after boot and note the line Control UI: http://…
  3. On your phone open that URL (try http://flighttracker.local/ first).

From there: On / Off, brightness slider, night schedule.
